# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

## Reflection

During this mission the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **AWS**. **AWS** offers a range of services and has a strong global infrastructure. I found **AWS** services such as Amazon EC2, S3, RDS and Lambda useful for different types of applications and business needs. **AWS** also provides tools that can help organizations build, manage and scale their systems.

**AWS**, Azure and GCP share similarities. All three offer cloud-based computing, storage, databases, networking, security and monitoring services. They also let organizations run virtual machines, store data, deploy applications and scale resources as demand changes. Each aims to provide reliable infrastructure so that businesses do not have to maintain all physical hardware.

Business requirements strongly influence the choice of a platform. A company has to consider its budget, application needs, security requirements, performance, scalability, existing technology and available technical skills. For example, a company already using Microsoft technologies may prefer Azure, while another organization may choose **AWS** or GCP because of specific services or pricing.

Working as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that picking a platform is not only about choosing the most popular provider. It requires understanding a customer's needs and matching them with the appropriate cloud services. I also learned how to compare platforms and suggest practical solutions.

Finally, this mission improved my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** by adding research, Linux investigation, cloud service comparisons, screenshots and reflections. It helped organize my work and showed my understanding of cloud computing concepts. Overall, the mission gave me confidence in analyzing cloud solutions and explaining why a particular platform may be suitable for a business.
